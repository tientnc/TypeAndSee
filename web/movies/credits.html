Sources and credits

Movie records

The starter catalogue combines excerpts from the CMU Movie Summary Corpus with 103 original premise descriptions for newer films. The CMU corpus was collected by David Bamman, Brendan O'Connor, and Noah A. Smith and contains Wikipedia plot summaries and Freebase metadata. Corpus material is used under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. The catalogue and its transformations are distributed under the same license. Wikipedia contributors are the authors of the underlying plot text. Each film's source link identifies its Wikipedia article, including its history of contributors.

Transformations: a subset was selected; plot excerpts were shortened; genres were grouped; movie titles, years, and runtimes were aligned; embeddings and a two-dimensional PCA projection were computed. Newer titles have original TypeAndSee premise descriptions with Wikipedia links and source-checked runtime metadata. Metadata can contain errors, and the starter catalogue is not a complete or current inventory of all films.

Research citation: David Bamman, Brendan O'Connor, and Noah A. Smith. Learning Latent Personas of Film Characters. ACL 2013. The institutions and source providers do not endorse this application.

Embeddings and vector search

Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, an ONNX conversion of Sentence Transformers' MiniLM model, supplies normalized 384-dimensional text embeddings. Transformers.js runs optional text queries in the browser. Orama indexes the catalogue vectors. The movie map uses a PCA projection; it is illustrative and distorts distances.

Privacy and access

Selected films and imported Letterboxd CSV records remain in local browser storage. This app does not log into or scrape Letterboxd and is not affiliated with Letterboxd, TMDB, Wikipedia, or the cited research groups. No paid model API is called by this demo. Model files and JavaScript libraries are downloaded from Hugging Face and jsDelivr; those services receive the network requests needed to serve their files.

Film-card artwork is original typographic styling, not studio posters. Source code and build scripts.
